The site's own caveat is that this ratio depends on a cost allocation, so the
video earns the right to show the ratio by first saying exactly what is on
each side of the divide. No blueprint fits a formula assembling term by term,
so this one is composed.

Scene 1 (0.0-1.6s): the numerator "Gross profit realised to date" writes on in Newsreader, ink, sitting on the left 60% of an asymmetric 60/40 split. Right rail stays empty paper.
Scene 2 (1.6-3.2s): a hairline division rule draws left to right beneath it, then the denominator "Acquisition cost" settles below the rule. The two terms now read as one fraction.
Scene 3 (3.2-4.8s): a mono tag "per logo" springs into the right rail, and a second "same cohort count on both sides" sets beneath it in ink-soft. This is the site's own like-for-like note and it belongs next to the formula, not in a footnote.
Scene 4 (4.8-8.0s): the formula slides up and compacts to a third of its size; beneath it a full-width ribbon of 24 hairline ticks draws left to right, the first labelled 2024-09 and the last 2026-08 in amber. Ticks arrive in one continuous sweep, then everything holds.

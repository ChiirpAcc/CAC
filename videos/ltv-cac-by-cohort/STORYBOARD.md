## Video direction

Silent by construction. There is no narration, no music and no SFX, so
**every `voiceover:` value below is on-screen copy, not spoken**. Pace each
reveal to reading time: a line holds long enough to be read once at an
unhurried pace, roughly 2.5 words per second, and the frame does not move
again while a new line is being read.

Look: `frame.md` (Cobalt Grid remixed onto the site's own palette). Cool grey
paper, near-black ink, a permanent hairline grid, Newsreader for display and
DM Mono for every figure. Numerals are the subject of this video, so they are
always mono and always tabular.

Colour carries meaning and is never decoration. Teal is the neutral series.
Green means at or above break-even, red means below it, grey means a cohort
too young to judge, amber is the accent for a label the eye must find. A
number never changes colour mid-frame unless its meaning changed.

Rhythm: frames 1, 5 and 7 are held beats. Frames 3, 4 and 6 carry the motion.
Frame 4 is the centrepiece and earns the longest build. Never move the camera
while a number is being read.

Framing runs centered → asymmetric 60/40 → triptych → full-width strip →
split-screen → asymmetric 70/30 → centered, so no two neighbouring frames
share a composition.

Honesty rule, inherited from the site: the falling right-hand side of this
chart must never be allowed to read as pure decay. Frame 4 shows the young
cohorts in grey and frame 6 says plainly what would make the whole reading
wrong.

- status: outline

- type: hook
- persuasion: Contrast, stated as two numbers and nothing else
- beat: attention
- blueprint: kinetic-type-beats (Reproduce)
- focal: the cost figure, set in mono at display-hero scale
- roles: cost figure = foreground subject · fixed lead line "A customer costs" = supporting · hairline grid = background (dim ~35%)
- poster: 4s
- src: compositions/frames/01-what-a-customer-costs.html

Open cold on the number, not on the topic. The chart's whole argument is a
price change, so the price change is the first thing on screen and the phrase
LTV:CAC does not appear yet.

Scene 1 (0.0-1.4s): hairline grid field settles. The lead line "A customer costs" sets upper-third left, Newsreader, ink. Nothing else on screen. Centered template, the type block holds ~55% of frame width.
Scene 2 (1.4-3.0s): "$2,583" springs in beneath the lead line at display-hero scale, DM Mono, teal, with a small "2024-10 cohort" micro label in amber beneath it. Per-character reveal, settling long-tail.
Scene 3 (3.0-4.4s): hard cut in place, the signature move: "$2,583" is replaced by "$9,367" on the same baseline, red, the micro label swapping to "2026-08 cohort". The old figure does not fade, it is cut away, so the swap reads as a substitution rather than a transition.
Scene 4 (4.4-6.0s): a thin amber bracket draws once between where the two numbers sat and a small mono tag "3.6x the price" settles at its midpoint. Everything then holds STILL for the read.

The site's own caveat is that this ratio depends on a cost allocation, so the
video earns the right to show the ratio by first saying exactly what is on
each side of the divide. No blueprint fits a formula assembling term by term,
so this one is composed.

Scene 1 (0.0-1.6s): the numerator "Gross profit realised to date" writes on in Newsreader, ink, sitting on the left 60% of an asymmetric 60/40 split. Right rail stays empty paper.
Scene 2 (1.6-3.2s): a hairline division rule draws left to right beneath it, then the denominator "Acquisition cost" settles below the rule. The two terms now read as one fraction.
Scene 3 (3.2-4.8s): a mono tag "per logo" springs into the right rail, and a second "same cohort count on both sides" sets beneath it in ink-soft. This is the site's own like-for-like note and it belongs next to the formula, not in a footnote.
Scene 4 (4.8-8.0s): the formula slides up and compacts to a third of its size; beneath it a full-width ribbon of 24 hairline ticks draws left to right, the first labelled 2024-09 and the last 2026-08 in amber. Ticks arrive in one continuous sweep, then everything holds.

The single most important frame in the video. A viewer who leaves with only
this one has the finding.

Scene 1 (0.0-1.6s): two cards enter from opposite wings with mirrored book-open tilts and settle side by side on a split-screen. Left card reads "2024-09 to 2025-05", right reads "2025-09 to 2026-08". Cards are hairline outline only, no fill, no shadow.
Scene 2 (1.6-3.6s): inside the left card a horizontal range bar draws from $2,583 to $4,920 in teal, its two endpoints labelled in mono.
Scene 3 (3.6-5.4s): the right card's range bar draws from $5,126 to $9,367 in red, on the same horizontal scale as the left, so its greater length is literal rather than styled.
Scene 4 (5.4-7.0s): the signature move, an inner-edge pill badge spring-pops on each card: "bought cheap" on the left, "bought dear" on the right, both mono, ink on paper.
Scene 5 (7.0-9.0s): beneath both cards a single Newsreader line writes on: "The price of a customer changed. The value of one did not." Held STILL to the cut.

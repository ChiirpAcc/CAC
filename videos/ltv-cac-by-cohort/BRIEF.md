## Intent

Chart 1 of the CHIIRP LTV:CAC site, explained as a short silent video an
operator can watch before reading the chart itself. The site is a diagnostic
read by operators, not a pitch deck, and the video inherits that: say the
finding, then say what would make it wrong.

The finding is not "unit economics are bad". It is narrower and more useful:
the earliest cohorts in the window returned two to nearly four times what they
cost, on roughly a third of the acquisition cost the newest ones carry. The
retention curve did not collapse. The price of buying a customer roughly
tripled. That points the lever at acquisition cost rather than at pricing or
upsell.

## Customizations

- Fully silent. No narration, no music, no SFX. Every word is on screen.
- On-screen text must be readable at its own pace; this is read, not heard.
- Numbers are live figures pulled from the deployed site on 2026-09-18.

## Notes

- House style from the repo: no em dashes. Say the finding, then say what
  would make it wrong.
- Realised, not projected: a young cohort sits low because it is young. The
  video must not let a viewer read the falling right-hand side as pure decay.
- Palette follows the site: cool grey paper, teal ink, amber, green, red.
